Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of Roshan Karki. It has one feature that handles personal information: a contact form. This page explains what happens to the information you send through it.

What I collect

When you submit the contact form, I receive and store:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the subject of your message
  • the message itself
  • the date and time you sent it

I do not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and I do not ask you to create an account.

How I use it

I use your details only to read your message and reply to you. I do not sell your information, share it for marketing, or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Messages are saved in a database (MongoDB Atlas) and are also sent to my personal email inbox. Only I have access to them. The website is hosted on Vercel, which processes requests on my behalf.

Google services

The site uses the Gmail API only to send the notification email about your message from my own Gmail account to my own inbox. It does not read, store, or access anyone else's Google account or Gmail data, and visitors are never asked to sign in with Google.

How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete them when they are no longer useful.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you the information I hold about you, or to delete it, at any time. Email me at roshankarki4956@gmail.com and I will respond as soon as I can.

Changes to this page

If I change how the contact form works, I will update this page and the date at the top.
